FILTER YF-19 - Windows (64-bit)
===============================

VST3 PLUGIN
  1. Copy the whole "Filter YF-19.vst3" folder to:
       C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
     (Windows will ask for administrator permission - click Continue.)
  2. In your DAW, rescan plugins. Filter YF-19 appears as an effect (Fx / Filter).
  3. Put it in the FX slot AFTER your synth (e.g. after Spire) so it filters the finished sound.

MIDI
  CC 74 = cutoff and CC 71 = resonance out of the box. Click MIDI LEARN to map anything else.
  Your DAW has to send MIDI to the effect - see README for Reaper / FL / Bitwig / Cubase / Ableton.

STANDALONE
  Double-click "Filter YF-19.exe". Options -> Audio/MIDI Settings to pick input, output and controller.
  Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" the first time (the app isn't code-signed):
  click "More info" -> "Run anyway".
